# Anchor Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025

## Plain English Summary

**What you can and cannot do:**
- **Can:** Use Anchor honestly to recover from gambling addiction.
- **Cannot:** Create multiple accounts, lie to the AI, bypass monitoring, harass your Guardian.
- **Cannot:** Use VPNs for offshore gambling, create new bank accounts to evade detection.
- **Cannot:** Abuse the system by spamming requests or attempting to hack it.

**If you violate this policy, we can reduce your allowance to $0 and suspend features (but your commitment period still continues).**

**Use Anchor as intended: honestly, transparently, and in pursuit of recovery.**

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines prohibited and permitted uses of the Anchor service. By using Anchor, you agree to comply with this AUP in addition to our Terms of Service.

## 2. Permitted Uses

### 2.1 Intended Use

Anchor is intended for:
- **Recovery from gambling addiction:** Using the service to overcome compulsive gambling
- **Financial protection:** Preventing access to money during vulnerability
- **Pattern awareness:** Understanding your gambling triggers and behaviors
- **Accountability:** Maintaining transparency with your Guardian
- **Honest engagement:** Truthfully responding to AI interventions

### 2.2 Acceptable Behavior

You may:
- Submit payment requests for legitimate expenses (groceries, bills, transport)
- Engage honestly with AI conversations
- Request human review of automated decisions
- Contact support for technical issues
- Export your data for personal records
- Update your contact information
- Communicate respectfully with your Guardian

## 3. Prohibited Uses

### 3.1 Account Violations

You must NOT:
- **Create multiple accounts:** One account per person (permanent ban for violations)
- **Share account credentials:** Never give login access to others
- **Impersonate others:** Account must be in your real name
- **Sell or transfer account:** Accounts are non-transferable
- **Access another user's account:** Even with their permission

### 3.2 Evasion and Manipulation

You must NOT:
- **Create new bank accounts to evade monitoring:** All your accounts must be connected
- **Use cash exclusively to avoid transactions:** Cash-only lifestyle defeats the purpose
- **Gamble using someone else's account:** Using a friend's Sportsbet account is still gambling
- **Use VPNs to access offshore gambling sites:** Detected via crypto exchange patterns
- **Transfer money to others for gambling on your behalf:** Detected via pattern analysis
- **Use crypto to fund offshore gambling:** CoinSpot/Binance purchases are flagged
- **Manipulate pattern detection:** Intentionally structuring transactions to avoid detection
- **Lie to the AI during interventions:** Manipulation tactics are detected and called out

### 3.3 System Abuse

You must NOT:
- **Spam payment requests:** More than 10 requests per hour is abuse
- **Repeatedly trigger emergency check-ins:** Misuse of Guardian emergency button
- **Attempt to bypass technical controls:** Hacking, reverse engineering, exploiting bugs
- **Denial-of-service attacks:** Flooding the system with requests
- **Automated requests:** Using bots or scripts to submit payment requests
- **SQL injection or XSS attacks:** Security testing without permission is prohibited

### 3.4 Harassment and Abuse

You must NOT:
- **Harass or threaten your Guardian:** Abusive behavior results in immediate suspension
- **Harass Anchor support staff:** Respectful communication required
- **Doxx or threaten other users:** (If we add community features)
- **Use offensive language in payment requests:** Profanity directed at AI is flagged
- **Abuse emergency services:** False claims of self-harm to manipulate the system

### 3.5 Fraudulent Activity

You must NOT:
- **Provide false information during onboarding:** Real name, accurate date of birth required
- **Submit fake invoices for payment approval:** Fraudulent receipts for payment requests
- **Claim fake emergencies:** Lying about medical emergencies to access Vault
- **Reverse chargebacks maliciously:** Disputing legitimate charges with Up Bank
- **Identity theft:** Using someone else's identity for your account
- **Money laundering:** Using Anchor to launder illicit funds

### 3.6 Unauthorized Access

You must NOT:
- **Access Up Bank accounts you don't own:** Only your own Up Bank account
- **Revoke API access to bypass monitoring:** Revoking Up Bank access violates Terms
- **Attempt to access Guardian portal:** Users cannot access Guardian-only features
- **Attempt to access admin dashboard:** Only authorized Anchor staff
- **Access other users' data:** Database queries attempting to access others' records

### 3.7 Misrepresentation

You must NOT:
- **Claim Anchor is medical treatment:** It's a financial tool, not therapy
- **Claim Anchor is financial advice:** We don't provide investment or financial planning
- **Misrepresent Anchor to your Guardian:** Don't downplay the seriousness of your gambling
- **Post false reviews:** Fake positive or negative reviews on app stores

## 4. Consequences of Violations

### 4.1 Warning

For minor first-time violations:
- **Email warning** detailing the violation
- **Requirement to acknowledge** understanding of the policy
- **Monitoring** for repeat violations

### 4.2 Daily Allowance Reduction

For repeated or serious violations:
- **Allowance reduced to $0** for 14 days (with notice)
- **Manual approval required** for all payment requests during reduction period
- **Guardian notified** of violation and reduction

### 4.3 Feature Suspension

For severe violations:
- **Voice payment requests disabled**
- **Automatic payment approval disabled** (all requests require AI conversation)
- **App features limited** to read-only mode

### 4.4 Account Suspension

For very severe violations:
- **Login access revoked**
- **Payment requests blocked** entirely
- **Guardian notified** of suspension
- **Vault remains locked** until commitment end date
- **Commitment period continues** (does NOT reset or shorten)

**Note:** Account suspension does NOT release you from your commitment. Your money stays locked until the original end date.

### 4.5 Permanent Ban

For extreme violations:
- **Account permanently banned**
- **Blacklisted from creating new accounts** (name, email, phone, device ID)
- **Data retained** for legal/fraud prevention purposes
- **Vault remains locked** until commitment end date (transferred to Up Bank control if Anchor is banned)
- **Law enforcement contacted** for criminal activity (fraud, threats, hacking)

### 4.6 Legal Action

Anchor reserves the right to pursue legal action for:
- Hacking attempts or unauthorized access
- Fraud or money laundering
- Threats or harassment
- Violations of Australian criminal law

## 5. Enforcement Process

### 5.1 Violation Detection

Violations are detected via:
- **Automated monitoring:** Pattern detection, spam detection, security scans
- **Guardian reports:** Guardians can report violations to support@anchor.app
- **Support ticket reviews:** Support staff review abusive or suspicious behavior
- **Third-party reports:** Up Bank, OpenAI, or other partners flagging violations

### 5.2 Investigation

When a violation is suspected:
- **Review of logs:** Transaction logs, AI conversation logs, API access logs
- **Evidence gathering:** Screenshots, conversation transcripts, pattern analysis
- **Determination:** Anchor determines if violation occurred and severity

### 5.3 Notification

If a violation is confirmed:
- **Email notification** within 7 days of detection
- **Explanation** of what policy was violated
- **Evidence** supporting the determination
- **Consequences** (warning, reduction, suspension, ban)
- **Appeal process** (detailed below)

### 5.4 Implementation

Consequences are implemented:
- **Warnings:** Immediate
- **Allowance reduction:** 14 days after notification (unless you appeal)
- **Suspension:** 7 days after notification (unless you appeal)
- **Ban:** Immediate for severe violations (threats, hacking)

## 6. Appeals Process

### 6.1 Right to Appeal

You can appeal any enforcement action except:
- Violations caught by automated security systems (hacking, SQL injection)
- Violations confirmed by video evidence or Guardian testimony
- Repeated violations (3+ warnings for same issue)

### 6.2 How to Appeal

To appeal an enforcement action:
- **Email:** appeals@anchor.app
- **Subject line:** "Appeal - [Your User ID] - [Violation Type]"
- **Include:** Detailed explanation why you believe the determination was incorrect
- **Evidence:** Any evidence supporting your appeal (screenshots, receipts, etc.)
- **Deadline:** Within 14 days of notification

### 6.3 Appeal Review

Anchor will review your appeal within:
- **Warnings:** 7 business days
- **Allowance reduction:** 14 business days
- **Suspension:** 14 business days
- **Ban:** 30 business days

Appeal is reviewed by a different staff member than the one who made the original determination.

### 6.4 Appeal Outcomes

Possible outcomes:
- **Overturned:** Violation determination reversed; consequences removed
- **Reduced:** Severity reduced (e.g., suspension reduced to warning)
- **Upheld:** Original determination stands; consequences proceed
- **Escalated:** Further investigation required; temporary hold on consequences

You'll be notified of the outcome via email with detailed reasoning.

## 7. Reporting Violations

### 7.1 Reporting Others

If you become aware of another user violating this AUP (e.g., in a future community feature), report to:
- **Email:** abuse@anchor.app
- **Include:** User ID (if known), description of violation, evidence (screenshots)

### 7.2 Reporting System Vulnerabilities

If you discover a security vulnerability:
- **DO:** Report to security@anchor.app immediately
- **DO NOT:** Exploit the vulnerability or share it publicly
- **Reward:** We may offer a bounty for responsible disclosure

### 7.3 Reporting Illegal Activity

If you witness illegal activity through Anchor:
- **Child abuse:** Contact NSW Police (131 444) and Anchor (abuse@anchor.app)
- **Money laundering:** Contact AUSTRAC (1300 021 037)
- **Other crimes:** Contact local law enforcement

## 8. Specific Use Case Guidance

### 8.1 Legitimate Emergency Access

**Permitted:**
- Requesting payment for genuine medical emergency (hospital, urgent care)
- Requesting payment for critical bills to avoid disconnection (power, water)
- Requesting payment for essential car repairs (to get to work)

**Required:**
- Voice payment request explaining urgency
- Documentation (invoice, bill, receipt) provided after the fact
- Honest AI conversation if triggered

### 8.2 Guardian Communication

**Permitted:**
- Discussing your progress with Guardian
- Asking Guardian to monitor your portal activity
- Requesting Guardian trigger emergency check-in if you're struggling

**Prohibited:**
- Pressuring Guardian to lie on your behalf
- Asking Guardian to help you bypass the system
- Harassing Guardian for not responding quickly enough

### 8.3 Pattern Detection Transparency

**Permitted:**
- Asking support why a pattern was detected
- Requesting human review of pattern classification
- Providing context for false positive transactions

**Prohibited:**
- Demanding patterns be removed from your record
- Claiming all detections are false positives
- Attempting to game the system based on detection criteria

## 9. Changes to This Policy

Anchor may update this AUP to address new violations, clarify policies, or comply with legal requirements.

**Notification:**
- 30 days notice via email
- In-app notification on next login
- Updated policy posted on website

**Acceptance:**
- Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance
- If you don't accept, you can terminate (if outside commitment period)

## 10. Definitions

**Evasion:** Intentional actions to avoid pattern detection or financial monitoring

**Manipulation:** Attempting to deceive the AI, Guardian, or Anchor staff

**Abuse:** Using the service in a way that harms Anchor, your Guardian, or other users

**Violation:** Breach of this Acceptable Use Policy or Terms of Service

**Pattern:** Behavioral indicator detected by automated analysis of transactions

**System:** Anchor's software, servers, databases, APIs, and mobile app

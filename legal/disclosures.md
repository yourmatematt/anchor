## 1. Not Medical Treatment

### 1.1 What Anchor Is

Anchor is a **financial restriction tool** designed to:
- Lock your money away during a commitment period
- Monitor your bank transactions for gambling patterns
- Trigger AI-powered conversations when patterns are detected
- Notify a trusted person (Guardian) of concerning behaviors

### 1.2 What Anchor Is NOT

**Anchor is NOT:**
- Medical treatment for gambling disorder
- Psychotherapy or counseling
- A substitute for professional mental health care
- A guarantee against gambling relapse
- A cure for gambling addiction
- Approved or endorsed by any medical body

### 1.3 Clinical Gambling Disorder

Gambling disorder is a recognized mental health condition in the DSM-5 (Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, 5th Edition).

**Diagnostic criteria include:**
- Preoccupation with gambling
- Need to gamble with increasing amounts
- Unsuccessful efforts to stop
- Restlessness when attempting to cut down
- Gambling to escape problems or relieve distress
- Chasing losses
- Lying to conceal extent of gambling
- Jeopardizing relationships, job, or education
- Relying on others for money

**If you meet 4+ criteria, you have a clinical gambling disorder and should seek professional help.**

### 1.4 Professional Treatment Required

Anchor **strongly recommends** you pursue professional treatment alongside using this service:

**Recommended treatments:**
- Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) with a psychologist specializing in gambling
- Support groups (Gamblers Anonymous Australia)
- Psychiatric evaluation (if depression, anxiety, or other mental health issues present)
- Financial counseling
- Relationship counseling (if gambling has damaged relationships)

**Find professional help:**
- **Gambling Help Online:** gamblinghelponline.org.au or 1800 858 858 (24/7)
- **Headspace:** headspace.org.au (for young people 12-25)
- **Beyond Blue:** beyondblue.org.au or 1300 22 4636
- **Your GP:** Ask for referral to psychologist (Mental Health Care Plan for Medicare rebates)

### 1.5 Anchor is a Complement, Not a Replacement

Think of Anchor as:
- **A lock on your wallet** - Not a therapist
- **A witness to your behavior** - Not a treatment plan
- **A barrier between you and money** - Not a cure for the underlying compulsion

**Best outcomes occur when Anchor is used WITH professional treatment, not instead of it.**

## 2. Not Financial Advice

### 2.1 What Anchor Does

Anchor restricts your access to money and monitors your spending. That's all.

### 2.2 What Anchor Does NOT Do

**Anchor does NOT:**
- Provide financial advice or financial planning
- Recommend investments
- Advise on debt management strategies
- Help you budget (beyond $30/day allowance)
- Negotiate with creditors
- Repair your credit score
- Provide tax advice
- Advise on bankruptcy or insolvency

### 2.3 Financial Advice Disclaimer

**Nothing in the Anchor service constitutes financial advice.** You should seek independent financial advice from a licensed financial advisor or counselor.

**If you're in financial distress:**
- **National Debt Helpline:** ndh.org.au or 1800 007 007 (free, confidential)
- **Financial Counselling Australia:** financialcounsellingaustralia.org.au
- **Your bank:** Most banks have hardship teams for customers in financial difficulty

### 2.4 Debt and Anchor

**Anchor does not solve debt problems.** Locking your money away does not erase debts.

If you have gambling-related debts:
1. Contact National Debt Helpline (1800 007 007) immediately
2. Do not take out payday loans to cover debts (400% interest makes it worse)
3. Consider debt consolidation or payment plans with creditors
4. Avoid bankruptcy unless absolutely necessary (seek professional advice first)

**Using Anchor while in severe debt may make your financial situation worse** if you cannot access money for essential payments.

## 3. Suicide Prevention Resources

### 3.1 If You're in Crisis

**If you're having thoughts of suicide or self-harm, Anchor is not a crisis intervention service.**

**Call these services immediately:**

**Lifeline (24/7 crisis support):**
- **Phone:** 13 11 14
- **Text:** 0477 13 11 14
- **Online chat:** lifeline.org.au

**Suicide Call Back Service (24/7):**
- **Phone:** 1300 659 467
- **Online counseling:** suicidecallbackservice.org.au

**Beyond Blue (24/7 mental health support):**
- **Phone:** 1300 22 4636
- **Online chat:** beyondblue.org.au

**Emergency (life-threatening situation):**
- **Call 000** and ask for ambulance

### 3.2 Gambling and Suicide Risk

**Statistics you should know:**
- Gambling disorder has the highest suicide rate of any addiction
- 1 in 5 problem gamblers attempt suicide
- Suicide risk increases with debt, relationship breakdown, and legal problems

**Warning signs:**
- Talking about wanting to die or feeling hopeless
- Researching suicide methods
- Giving away possessions
- Saying goodbye to loved ones
- Sudden calmness after period of depression (may indicate decision made)

**If you notice these signs in yourself or someone else, GET HELP IMMEDIATELY.**

### 3.3 Anchor's Role in Crisis

If you express suicidal ideation in an AI conversation:
- Anchor will **immediately provide crisis resources**
- Anchor will **notify your Guardian** (if applicable and safe to do so)
- Anchor will **encourage you to call Lifeline**
- Anchor **does NOT have trained crisis counselors** and cannot talk you down

**The AI is not qualified to handle suicidal ideation. It will direct you to appropriate services.**

### 3.4 Guardian Emergency Protocol

If you're a Guardian and the User expresses suicidal thoughts:
1. **Take it seriously** - Do not dismiss as attention-seeking
2. **Call 000** if you believe there's imminent risk
3. **Encourage them to call Lifeline** (13 11 14)
4. **Do not leave them alone** if you're with them in-person
5. **Remove means of self-harm** if possible (secure medications, firearms, etc.)
6. **Follow up** - Check on them daily for at least a week

**You are not responsible for preventing their suicide, but you can help them access resources.**

## 4. Automated Decision-Making Disclosure

### 4.1 AI-Powered System

Anchor uses artificial intelligence (OpenAI GPT-4) to:
- Analyze your transactions
- Detect gambling patterns
- Conduct intervention conversations
- Determine if you're being truthful
- Approve or deny payment requests (in some cases)

### 4.2 How Automated Decisions Work

**Pattern Detection:**
- Algorithms classify transactions based on merchant name, amount, time, frequency
- Risk scores (0-100) are calculated using predefined formulas
- Patterns are detected when multiple indicators align (e.g., Tuesday + transfer + round number)
- Confidence scores indicate likelihood of gambling (e.g., 85% confident Tuesday poker pattern)

**AI Conversations:**
- GPT-4 generates responses based on conversation context
- Manipulation detection uses pattern matching against 10 known tactics
- Conversation outcomes (honest admission vs. evasion) determined by sentiment analysis

**Payment Approval:**
- Requests under $30 with no red flags are auto-approved
- Requests with flags (round number, vague reason, late night) require AI conversation
- AI can approve if reason is deemed legitimate, or deny if suspicious

### 4.3 Limitations of AI

**AI can make mistakes:**
- **False positives:** Legitimate transactions flagged as gambling (e.g., ATM at shopping center)
- **False negatives:** Gambling transactions not detected (e.g., new offshore site not in database)
- **Misinterpretation:** AI may misunderstand context in conversations
- **Bias:** AI trained on patterns may not account for your unique circumstances

**AI is not perfect. It's a tool, not a judge of character.**

### 4.4 Your Rights

Under GDPR Article 22, you have the right to:
- **Not be subject solely to automated decision-making** with significant effects
- **Request human review** of automated decisions
- **Receive an explanation** of how automated decisions were made
- **Challenge decisions** you believe are incorrect

**To request human review:**
- Email support@anchor.app with decision ID (from app notification)
- Human reviewer (Anchor staff member) will review within 7 business days
- Human reviewer can override automated decision

### 4.5 Transparency Commitment

Anchor commits to:
- Showing you why patterns were detected (indicators, confidence score)
- Explaining why payment requests were flagged
- Publishing pattern detection criteria in documentation
- Regular audits of AI decision accuracy

## 5. Data Retention Policies

### 5.1 What Data We Keep

Anchor retains:
- **Transaction history:** 7 years (Australian regulatory requirement for financial records)
- **AI conversation transcripts:** 7 years
- **Pattern detection logs:** 7 years
- **Payment request history:** 7 years
- **Personal information:** 90 days after commitment period ends
- **Voice recordings:** 24 hours only, then permanently deleted

### 5.2 Why We Keep Data

**Legal requirements:**
- Australian Taxation Office (ATO) requires financial records retained for 7 years
- Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act (AML/CTF) requirements
- Potential tax audits or investigations

**Legitimate business purposes:**
- Fraud prevention and detection
- Pattern analysis and service improvement
- Dispute resolution (if you claim we made an error)
- Legal defense (if you sue us)

### 5.3 Cannot Delete During Commitment

**You cannot delete your data during your commitment period.** This is essential to the service:
- Deleting transaction history would prevent pattern detection
- Deleting conversation logs would prevent manipulation detection
- Deleting patterns would remove accountability

**After your commitment period ends,** you can request deletion (see Section 5.5).

### 5.4 Anonymization After 7 Years

After 7 years:
- Personal identifiers removed (name, email, phone, date of birth)
- User ID replaced with anonymous hash
- Data used for aggregate analytics only (e.g., "30% of users with payday loans relapse within 60 days")
- Cannot be re-identified or linked back to you

### 5.5 Account Deletion Process

**After commitment period ends:**
1. Email privacy@anchor.app requesting account deletion
2. Confirm via SMS code (to verify it's really you)
3. 30-day waiting period (in case you change your mind)
4. Personal information deleted (name, email, phone)
5. Financial records anonymized (retained 7 years, per legal requirements)
6. Confirmation email sent when deletion complete

**During commitment period:**
- Account deletion requests are denied
- You can request after commitment end date

## 9. Risk Disclosure

### 9.1 Financial Risks

**Risks of using Anchor:**
- **Inability to access money in emergencies:** Your Vault is locked even for genuine emergencies
- **$30/day may not be enough:** If your living costs exceed $30/day, you may struggle
- **Manual transfers take time:** Payments over $50 require manual transfer (1-3 business days)
- **No hardship provisions:** Even if you lose your job, Vault stays locked

**Mitigation:**
- Ensure $30/day covers your absolute essentials before committing
- Build emergency fund before starting Anchor (not in the Vault)
- Consider shorter commitment period if you're unsure (90 days minimum)

### 9.2 Mental Health Risks

**Risks of confrontational AI:**
- **Emotional distress:** The AI is designed to be direct and may be upsetting
- **Increased cravings:** Talking about gambling may trigger urges
- **Shame and guilt:** Facing your patterns can be painful
- **Relationship strain:** Guardian seeing your struggles may cause friction

**Mitigation:**
- Have a therapist or counselor before starting Anchor
- Use crisis resources (Lifeline: 13 11 14) if the process becomes overwhelming
- Take breaks from the app if needed (commitment continues, but you don't have to check constantly)

### 9.3 Relationship Risks

**Risks to Guardian relationship:**
- **Resentment:** You may resent your Guardian for witnessing your struggles
- **Burden:** Guardian may feel overwhelmed by responsibility
- **Breakup:** If your Guardian is a partner, relationship breakdown makes the role complicated
- **Privacy tension:** Feeling watched can strain relationships

**Mitigation:**
- Choose a Guardian who truly understands addiction recovery
- Set boundaries with Guardian (they're witness, not police)
- Have backup Guardian in mind in case relationship changes

### 9.4 Technical Risks

**Risks of technology dependence:**
- **App bugs:** Software bugs may cause false detections or failed notifications
- **API outages:** Up Bank or OpenAI downtime disrupts service
- **Phone loss:** Losing your phone may prevent access to payment requests
- **Account lockout:** Forgetting password may delay support resolution

**Mitigation:**
- Keep alternative payment method for absolute emergencies (small amount of cash)
- Store backup contact methods for Anchor support
- Enable biometric login (Face ID/Touch ID) to prevent lockout

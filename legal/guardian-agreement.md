# Anchor Guardian Agreement

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025

## Plain English Summary

**What you're agreeing to as a Guardian:**
- You're a witness, not a controller. You see patterns, not dollar amounts.
- You cannot approve or deny their money requests.
- You'll get alerts when they're struggling, but you can't stop the system.
- You must keep what you see confidential.
- You're their emergency contact, but you can't cancel their commitment.
- This role lasts their entire commitment period (6-24 months). You cannot quit early.

**This is about accountability, not control. They chose you because they trust you to witness their journey.**

---

## 1. Acceptance of Role

By accepting the Guardian role, you ("Guardian", "you", "your") agree to monitor and support the User in their gambling recovery journey. You are not assuming legal, financial, or medical responsibility for the User.

### 1.1 Guardian Eligibility

To serve as a Guardian, you must:
- Be at least 18 years of age
- Not be the User (cannot be the same person)
- Have a relationship with the User (partner, family member, close friend)
- Be aware of the User's gambling problem
- Have a valid Australian mobile phone number for SMS notifications
- Agree to these terms for the duration of the User's commitment period

### 1.2 Acceptance Process

You accept this Agreement by:
- Receiving an invitation from the User via SMS
- Clicking the magic link in the invitation
- Reviewing this Guardian Agreement
- Confirming acceptance via the Guardian portal
- Providing your contact information

## 2. Guardian Role Definition

### 2.1 What You ARE

You are:
- **A witness:** You observe their progress and setbacks
- **Accountability:** Your visibility helps them stay honest
- **Emergency contact:** You're notified when they're in crisis
- **Support person:** You provide encouragement and understanding
- **Pattern monitor:** You see concerning behaviors develop

### 2.2 What You Are NOT

You are NOT:
- **A controller:** You cannot control their money or decisions
- **An approver:** You cannot approve or deny payment requests
- **A therapist:** You're not responsible for their mental health treatment
- **A financial advisor:** You're not managing their finances
- **A police officer:** You're not enforcing rules or consequences
- **A savior:** You cannot prevent all relapses

### 2.3 Your Commitment

By accepting this role, you commit to:
- **Monitoring:** Regularly checking the Guardian portal (at least weekly)
- **Responding:** Responding to emergency notifications within 24 hours
- **Confidentiality:** Not sharing their data with others (except in emergencies)
- **Non-interference:** Not attempting to manipulate or bypass the system
- **Duration:** Serving the full commitment period (you cannot quit early)

## 3. Guardian Visibility

### 3.1 What You CAN See

You have access to:
- **Clean streak:** Days since last gambling relapse
- **Pattern summaries:** Types of concerning patterns detected
  - Example: "Late night cash withdrawal pattern detected"
  - Example: "Recurring Tuesday night transfer pattern"
  - Example: "Payday loan transaction detected"
- **AI conversation status:** When conversations are triggered (not full transcripts)
  - Example: "AI intervention triggered: Gambling venue detected"
  - Example: "Conversation outcome: Honest admission"
- **Relapse notifications:** When a confirmed relapse occurs
  - Example: "Relapse recorded on Jan 15, 2025"
  - Example: "Clean streak reset to 0 days"
- **Progress trends:** Weekly summaries of patterns and interventions
- **Last activity timestamp:** When they last used the app
- **Commitment period:** Start date, end date, days remaining

### 3.2 What You CANNOT See

You do NOT have access to:
- **Dollar amounts:** Transaction values, vault balance, daily allowance usage
- **Transaction descriptions:** Specific merchants or purchase details
- **Full AI transcripts:** Complete conversation logs (only summaries)
- **Payment request details:** What they're asking for or why
- **Account credentials:** Up Bank access, passwords, API tokens
- **Location data:** Where transactions occurred

**This privacy boundary protects the User's dignity while maintaining accountability.**

### 3.3 Why This Visibility Matters

Limited visibility is intentional:
- **Patterns over amounts:** Behavior matters more than dollars
- **Dignity preservation:** They're not a child; you're not a parent
- **Focus on recovery:** Recovery isn't about money, it's about honesty
- **Reduced shame:** They're more honest when not judged on amounts

## 4. Guardian Limitations

### 4.1 What You CANNOT Do

You cannot:
- **Approve or deny transactions:** Only the AI and automated system decide
- **Access their money:** You have no control over their accounts
- **Modify their commitment period:** Cannot shorten or extend
- **Change their daily allowance:** Fixed at $30/day
- **Remove yourself as Guardian:** Must serve full commitment period
- **Cancel their commitment:** Only they can terminate (after commitment ends)
- **Bypass pattern detection:** Cannot whitelist transactions
- **View deleted data:** Voice recordings are deleted after 24 hours
- **Request account access:** Cannot log in as them

### 4.2 What You SHOULD NOT Do

Best practices:
- **Don't micromanage:** Don't demand explanations for every pattern
- **Don't shame them:** Relapses are part of recovery, not failures
- **Don't withhold support:** Even if you're frustrated, stay engaged
- **Don't share their data:** Respect confidentiality (except emergencies)
- **Don't make it about you:** This is their journey, not yours

## 5. Notification Responsibilities

### 5.1 When You'll Be Notified

You receive SMS notifications for:
- **High-risk patterns detected** (gambling online, payday loans, late-night cash)
- **AI conversation triggered** (intervention required)
- **Conversation timed out** (they didn't complete intervention within 10 minutes)
- **Relapse recorded** (confirmed gambling activity)
- **Streak milestones** (7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 6 months, 1 year)
- **Weekly progress reports** (Sunday 6pm AEST)
- **System errors** (Up Bank connection lost, technical issues)

### 5.2 Response Expectations

When notified:
- **Check the Guardian portal** within 24 hours
- **Read the pattern summary** to understand what happened
- **Consider reaching out** to the User (text, call, in-person)
- **Do not demand explanations** or shame them
- **Offer support** without judgment

### 5.3 Emergency Response

If you receive an emergency notification:
- **"Payday loan detected"** → Indicates severe financial stress, high relapse risk
- **"Multiple interventions failed"** → They're in crisis, consider welfare check
- **"Conversation abandoned repeatedly"** → They may be avoiding accountability

In true emergencies (suicide risk, self-harm):
- **Call 000** immediately
- Contact **Lifeline: 13 11 14**
- Contact **Beyond Blue: 1300 22 4636**
- Use the Guardian portal emergency button to trigger immediate AI check-in

## 6. Confidentiality Requirements

### 6.1 Your Obligations

You must:
- **Keep their data confidential:** Do not share with others (except in emergencies)
- **Secure your access:** Protect your Guardian portal login (magic link)
- **Not screenshot or record:** Do not capture portal data
- **Respect their privacy:** Even from mutual friends/family

### 6.2 Permitted Disclosures

You may disclose User information to:
- **Emergency services (000)** if there's imminent risk of harm
- **Mental health professionals** if the User consents
- **Lifeline/Beyond Blue** crisis counselors in genuine emergencies
- **Law enforcement** if legally compelled by court order

### 6.3 Breaches of Confidentiality

If you breach confidentiality:
- The User may request a replacement Guardian (after 7 days notice)
- Anchor may revoke your Guardian access
- You may be liable for damages under Australian privacy law

### 6.4 Exceptions for Safety

Confidentiality does NOT apply when:
- The User expresses intent to harm themselves or others
- You become aware of child abuse or neglect
- You are compelled by a valid court order
- The User has provided explicit written consent

## 7. Emergency Contact Responsibilities

### 7.1 Emergency Check-In Power

You can trigger an emergency AI check-in if:
- You're concerned about their welfare
- They're not responding to your messages
- You've observed warning signs (isolation, stress, desperation)
- You suspect a relapse has occurred
- They've missed significant events or obligations

### 7.2 How to Trigger Emergency Check-In

Via the Guardian portal:
1. Click "Emergency Check-In" button
2. Provide a brief reason (e.g., "Welfare check - not responding to texts")
3. Confirm you understand this triggers an AI intervention
4. The User receives a push notification requiring immediate response

### 7.3 Emergency Check-In Outcome

When triggered:
- The User must complete an AI conversation within 1 hour
- They're asked about their current state, recent activity, and intentions
- If they don't respond, you're notified and can escalate to 000
- If they complete the conversation, you see a summary (honest/evasive/concerning)

### 7.4 Do Not Abuse This Power

Emergency check-ins are for genuine concern, not:
- Checking up because you're curious
- Punishing them for not responding quickly
- Micromanaging their daily activities
- Testing the system

Abuse of emergency check-ins may result in removal as Guardian.

## 8. Guardian Tenure

### 8.1 Duration of Role

You serve as Guardian for:
- The User's entire commitment period (90-730 days)
- Plus 7 days post-commitment for final reporting
- Until a replacement Guardian is designated (if applicable)

### 8.2 Cannot Be Removed During Commitment

You cannot resign, quit, or be removed during the User's commitment period, except:
- **Mutual agreement:** Both you and the User agree to replacement
- **Your incapacity:** You become mentally or physically incapable
- **Your death:** Automatic removal
- **Restraining order:** Legal order prevents contact
- **Severe breach:** You violate confidentiality or manipulate the system

Replacement requires:
- 7 days notice
- User must designate a new Guardian
- New Guardian must accept before you're released
- Anchor must approve the replacement

### 8.3 After Commitment Period

After the User's commitment period ends:
- You continue receiving notifications for 7 days (transition period)
- The User can remove you immediately without replacement
- You can resign by giving 7 days notice
- Access to historical data is revoked after 30 days

## 9. Limitations of Liability

### 9.1 No Liability for Relapses

You are NOT liable for:
- The User's gambling relapses
- Financial losses from their gambling
- Consequences of their decisions
- AI conversation outcomes
- System failures or technical errors
- Their failure to complete interventions

### 9.2 No Legal Obligation to Intervene

You have no legal duty to:
- Physically prevent gambling
- Monitor them in-person
- Manage their finances
- Provide therapy or counseling
- Guarantee their recovery

### 9.3 Indemnification

Anchor indemnifies you from claims by the User or third parties arising from:
- Your good-faith performance as Guardian
- Notifications sent in genuine emergency
- Confidential information disclosed in true emergencies
- Decisions to trigger emergency check-ins

This indemnification does NOT cover:
- Intentional breaches of confidentiality
- Malicious use of emergency check-ins
- Fraud or criminal activity
- Violations of this Agreement

## 10. Data and Privacy

### 10.1 Your Data

Anchor collects from you:
- Name and relationship to User
- Mobile phone number
- Email address (optional)
- Portal activity logs
- Emergency check-in history

### 10.2 Data Retention

Your data is retained:
- Duration of User's commitment period
- Plus 90 days post-commitment
- Then anonymized or deleted (at User's request)

### 10.3 Your Privacy Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, you have the right to:
- Access your data (request a copy)
- Correct inaccurate data
- Complain about privacy breaches
- Understand how your data is used

### 10.4 User's Access to Your Data

The User can see:
- Your name and relationship
- When you last viewed the Guardian portal
- When you triggered emergency check-ins (and why)
- Your contact information (phone number)

The User CANNOT see:
- Your portal activity logs
- What specific patterns you viewed
- Your email communications with Anchor

## 11. Termination

### 11.1 Termination by Mutual Agreement

You and the User can mutually agree to replace you if:
- Your relationship has changed (e.g., breakup, falling out)
- You're no longer able to fulfill the role
- A more suitable Guardian is available
- Both parties agree to the change

Process:
- User designates replacement Guardian
- Replacement Guardian accepts
- You confirm agreement to step down
- Anchor approves transition
- 7-day handover period

### 11.2 Termination by Anchor

Anchor may remove you immediately if you:
- Breach confidentiality maliciously
- Attempt to manipulate or bypass the system
- Harass or abuse the User
- Violate these terms
- Pose a safety risk to the User

### 11.3 Automatic Termination

Your role automatically ends if:
- The User's commitment period ends and they don't renew
- The User's account is terminated
- You become subject to a restraining order preventing contact
- You pass away

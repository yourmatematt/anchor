### 5.2 Payment Requests

- Submit a payment request via the Anchor app
- Provide a reason (text or voice)

### 5.3 Automatic Triggers

Payment requests are automatically flagged if:
- Amount exceeds your remaining daily allowance
- Reason is vague ("need money", "for stuff")

- Request made between 10pm-4am
- Voice stress analysis indicates deception

Flagged requests require an AI conversation before approval or denial.

## 6. AI Intervention

### 6.1 Automated Decision-Making

Anchor uses artificial intelligence (OpenAI GPT-4) to:
- Analyze your transaction patterns
- Detect gambling behavior
- Conduct intervention conversations
- Determine if you are being truthful
- Decide whether to approve or deny payment requests
- Detect manipulation tactics

**You agree to interact with the AI when triggered.**

### 6.2 Intervention Triggers

AI conversations are triggered by:
- Direct gambling transactions (Sportsbet, Crown Casino)
- Payday loan transactions
- Detected patterns (Tuesday poker, late-night escalation)
- Flagged payment requests
- Guardian emergency check-in
- Multiple failed intervention attempts

### 6.3 Conversation Requirements

During AI conversations, you must:
- Respond honestly within 10 minutes (or conversation times out)
- Not attempt to manipulate or deceive the AI
- Accept that the AI may call out manipulation tactics
- Complete the conversation before payment approval

The AI is trained to detect:
- Minimization ("just a small bet")
- Deflection ("can we talk about something else")
- Rationalization ("I had a system")
- Blame shifting ("I was stressed")
- False promises ("last time, I swear")
- Partial truths ("just helping a friend")
- Future focus ("let's move forward")
- Appeals to pity ("going through a tough time")
- Attacking ("you don't understand")
- Playing victim ("everyone's against me")

**If you lie or manipulate, the AI will confront you directly.**

### 6.4 Conversation Outcomes

AI conversations can result in:
- **Approved:** Payment or transaction accepted as legitimate
- **Denied:** Payment request rejected; transaction recorded as relapse
- **Follow-up Required:** Additional verification needed
- **Timed Out:** Conversation abandoned; Guardian notified

### 6.5 Voice Recording

When you submit voice payment requests:
- Your voice is transcribed using OpenAI Whisper
- Transcription is analyzed for authenticity
- Voice recordings are deleted after 24 hours
- Transcripts are retained for pattern analysis

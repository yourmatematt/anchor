## Description

### Short Description (80 characters for Android)
Financial accountability for problem gamblers. Lock your money for 6-24 months.

### Full Description

**For people who've lost everything to gambling and need hard accountability.**

Anchor is NOT another gambling blocker. It's a financial lock that controls your money so you can't gamble with it.

## Requirements

- Up Bank account
- Someone to be your guardian
- Ready to commit for 6-24 months
- Hit rock bottom and want out

## How It Works

**1. Lock In**
Choose your commitment period. No cancel button. You're locked in.

**2. Daily Allowance**
Get $30 per day for essentials. Everything else goes to your locked vault.

**3. AI Interrogation**
Want more money? Have a voice conversation with AI. It knows your bullshit.

**4. Guardian Oversight**
Someone you trust sees when you're struggling (not your bank balance, just patterns).

**5. No Escape**
Can't disable biometrics. Can't dismiss alerts. Can't cancel commitment. That's the point.

## What Makes Anchor Different

❌ **Not gentle** - No soft talk, no comfort
❌ **Not optional** - Interventions cannot be skipped
❌ **Not temporary** - Lock in for months, not days
✅ **Actually works** - Because you can't escape it

## App Review Notes for Apple/Google

**This app is for people with gambling addiction.**

The "harsh" language and "no escape" features are intentional and necessary:

1. **Biometric lock required** - Prevents self-sabotage during commitment
2. **No cancel button** - Core feature, not a bug
3. **Mandatory notifications** - For intervention, can't be disabled
4. **Voice recording** - Only for AI conversations, transcribed and deleted
5. **Guardian monitoring** - With user consent during onboarding

**Not a gambling app.** This is an intervention tool that monitors Up Bank transactions to detect and prevent gambling.

**Australian focus:** Designed for Australian users with Up Bank accounts. Crisis resources are Australian services.

**Medical disclaimer included:** App clearly states it's not a substitute for professional counseling.

**Test credentials:** Will provide test Up Bank sandbox account for review.

# Anchor Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025

## Introduction

Anchor is a financial accountability app for people struggling with gambling addiction. We're brutally honest about what we do with your data because transparency is part of accountability.

## The Straight Answer

**We collect your transaction data to stop you from gambling.**

If that makes you uncomfortable, this app isn't for you. Anchor works by monitoring your money. That's the entire point.

## What We Collect

### Financial Data (via Up Bank API)
- **Transaction history:** Every debit and credit from your Up Bank account
- **Transaction details:** Merchant name, amount, timestamp, description
- **Account balances:** Current balance, available funds
- **Personal info from Up Bank:** Name, account number

**Why:** To detect gambling patterns and trigger interventions.

**Retention:** Last 90 days of transactions stored. Older data deleted automatically.

### AI Conversation Data
- **Voice recordings:** Transcribed and deleted within 24 hours
- **Conversation transcripts:** All text exchanges with the AI
- **Manipulation flags:** When you try to lie to the AI
- **Outcomes:** Approved, denied, confirmed gambling

**Why:** To make better decisions about your money requests. To prove to you (later) what you said when desperate.

**Retention:** Stored for duration of commitment period + 12 months.

### Guardian Visibility Data
- **Patterns detected:** Type, severity, timestamp (NO amounts)
- **Clean streak:** Days since last gambling
- **Conversation status:** Active, completed, outcome
- **Emergency triggers:** When guardian requests check-in

**Why:** Your guardian needs to see when you're struggling without invading your privacy.

**What they DON'T see:** Transaction amounts, account balances, specific spending.

### Device & Security Data
- **Biometric authentication:** Face ID/Touch ID (stored on device only)
- **Push notification tokens:** To send critical alerts
- **Device info:** OS version, app version, device model
- **Location:** Never collected

### Usage & Analytics
- **Anonymous app usage:** Which screens you visit, how long
- **Crash reports:** Anonymous technical errors
- **Feature usage:** Which features you use

**Why:** To make the app better and fix bugs.

## What We DON'T Collect

- ❌ **Location data** - We don't track where you are
- ❌ **Contacts** - We don't access your contacts
- ❌ **Photos/media** - Only voice recordings for AI conversations
- ❌ **Social media** - We don't connect to social accounts
- ❌ **Gambling history before Anchor** - Only what happens after you start

## How We Use Your Data

### To Stop You Gambling
- Detect gambling transactions and patterns
- Trigger AI interventions
- Block or delay money requests
- Alert your guardian when necessary

### To Help You Recover
- Track clean streaks and progress
- Calculate money saved vs. baseline
- Identify triggers and high-risk times
- Provide insights for therapy

### To Communicate
- Push notifications for interventions (cannot be disabled)
- Optional check-in reminders
- Weekly progress reports

### NOT for Advertising
We **never**:
- Sell your data
- Share with advertisers
- Use for marketing
- Track across other apps

## Data Sharing

### With Your Guardian (Limited)
Your guardian sees:
- ✅ Gambling patterns detected (type, severity)
- ✅ Clean streak days
- ✅ AI conversation status and outcomes
- ✅ When you're struggling

Your guardian does NOT see:
- ❌ Transaction amounts
- ❌ Account balances
- ❌ Where you spend money
- ❌ Specific merchant details

### With Service Providers
We use these third parties (minimum necessary data):
- **Up Bank:** Banking integration (already have your data)
- **Supabase:** Encrypted database hosting (Australian servers)
- **OpenAI:** AI conversation processing (transcripts only, deleted after 30 days per OpenAI policy)
- **Expo/Apple/Google:** Push notifications (device tokens only)
- **Sentry:** Anonymous crash reporting

### With Law Enforcement
Only if legally required (court order, subpoena). We'll fight it.

### NEVER Sold
We will never sell your data. Ever. Not even if offered money. That would defeat the purpose of Anchor.

## Data Security

### Encryption
- **In transit:** All API calls use TLS 1.3
- **At rest:** Database encrypted with AES-256
- **On device:** Biometric data stays on device, tokens in iOS Keychain/Android Keystore

### Access Control
- **Staff access:** Minimal. Only for technical support with user consent.
- **API security:** JWT tokens, rate limiting, input validation
- **No backdoors:** We can't access your Up Bank account ourselves

### Australian Data Residency
All data stored on Australian servers (Supabase Sydney region). Never leaves Australia.

## Your Rights

### Access Your Data
Request full export of your data: contact@anchor.app (response within 7 days)

### Delete Your Data
**During commitment period:** Cannot delete transaction/pattern data (that's the point)

**After commitment ends:** Full deletion within 30 days of request

**Account deletion:** Removes all data except:
- Guardian notification log (anonymized, 12 months)
- Aggregated anonymous statistics

### Correct Your Data
Contact us to fix incorrect information.

### Export Your Data
JSON export of all data we hold about you.

## Children's Privacy

**Age requirement:** 18+

Anchor is for adults with gambling problems. We don't knowingly collect data from minors.

## Changes to This Policy

We'll notify you of significant changes via:
- Push notification
- Email (if provided)
- In-app banner

You can reject changes by deleting your account (unless in commitment period).

## California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you're a California resident, you have rights under CCPA:
- Right to know what data we collect
- Right to delete your data
- Right to opt-out of data sales (we don't sell data)
- Right to non-discrimination

## European Users (GDPR)

If you're in the EU/UK (unlikely for an Australian app), you have GDPR rights:
- Right to access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object

**Legal basis for processing:** Legitimate interest (preventing gambling harm)

## Cookies

We don't use cookies in the mobile app. The marketing website uses:
- Google Analytics (anonymous, IP anonymized)
- No tracking/advertising cookies

## Third-Party Links

Crisis resources in the app (Gambling Help, Lifeline) link to external sites. Their privacy policies apply.

## Automated Decision Making

**Yes, Anchor uses automated decision making.**

The AI evaluates payment requests automatically. This is the core feature.

**You can challenge decisions:** Voice conversation with AI, then guardian notification if still denied.

## International Transfers

Data stays in Australia. If you're outside Australia using this app, your data is transferred to Australia.

## Business Transfers

If Anchor is acquired, your data transfers to new owner with same privacy commitments. You'll be notified 30 days before transfer with option to delete account.

## The Bottom Line

Anchor monitors your money to stop you gambling. That requires data collection. We're transparent about it. We don't sell it. We encrypt it. We let you export it. We delete it when you're done.

If you're not comfortable with this level of monitoring, Anchor won't work for you. That's okay. Call Gambling Help instead: 1800 858 858.

This is your last shot. Make it count.
